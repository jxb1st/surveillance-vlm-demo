Decision layer

Same 120 ADC items (VALU's Anomaly Description Choice task: pick the correct description of the video among the options), same 32 frames, same Qwen3.8-27B. What changes is how the decision is read out, plus a real per-frame edge trigger on the same videos.

Summary

  • Reading the VLM's own option probabilities (D1) keeps the generated answer's accuracy (119/120 identical), runs 4.8× faster and gives usable confidence: 97 % accuracy on the 56 % most confident items.
  • A separate zero-shot text decision model (D2) drops Matching to chance (12.5–17.5 %).
  • Fine-tuned on VALU (D3) it scores 95 / 100 / 100 % — and 92.5 / 100 / 100 % with no description at all; a 16k-parameter bag-of-words model over the option texts scores 97.5 %. The benchmark's options give the answer away; D3 is not evidence of video understanding.
  • Edge trigger: nothing trained on ETRI transfers to this footage (frame AUC 0.43–0.58 vs 0.64 for frame differencing); Qwen per frame ranks best (0.77) but cannot meet a 1 % false-positive budget.
  • Real trigger replay: one early alarm, one miss, one false alarm out of three cases.

Edge trigger

Every evaluation video at 1 fps, scored from the frames alone. Ground truth: 48 videos with abnormal spans (50 events) and 40 normal videos. Recall at 1 % / 5 % FPR = events detected when at most that fraction of normal seconds is under alarm (threshold chosen per detector on these videos). Alarm windows: onset − 10 s to + 20 s.

Protocol notes and run ids
  • Protocol valu_demo_v1_decision: the frozen manifest, cached frames and ADC prompt of the generative runs; only the readout differs. D0–D2 are not trained. D3 is trained on VALU items from videos disjoint from the evaluation and smoke sets (manifest valu-demo-v1-decision-train, 10 % of videos held out).
  • D2/D3 Laya budgets: max_len 1024, head_max_len 400 (checkpoint defaults raised so all options fit; no item was cut).
  • Edge thresholds are operating points chosen on the evaluation videos at matched false-positive rates, not deployment settings.
  • Runs: D0 generative = evaluation-qwen38_27b-9e291ee519; D1 letter logits = evaluation-qwen38_27b+letters-04b42426c0; D2 Laya typed-decisions = evaluation-qwen38_27b+laya_td-f6b42401e8; D2′ Laya root = evaluation-qwen38_27b+laya_root-c348c93694; D3 Laya fine-tuned (VALU) = evaluation-qwen38_27b+laya_ftv-db238237c1; replay = replay-qwen38_27b-af4768d4e0, replay_edge-qwen38_27b-cc96632dc2.
