Where should the decision live?

A surveillance pipeline watches the camera with a lightweight edge trigger, uploads a short window when it fires, and lets a Video VLM on the server decide what happened. We ask where in this chain the decision should be made — at the edge, inside the VLM, or in a separate decision model after it — and measure every option on the same frozen benchmark footage and on real CCTV.

What was tested

FootageVALU demo subset v1: 174 videos from UCF-Crime and ECVA (public media), 120 ADC / 60 TDG / 12 ALD items, frozen before any model ran; 2,651 further ADC items on 409 other videos for the scale check. Edge: 88 labelled videos (50 events, 40 normal videos) at 1 fps. ETRI CCTV: 125 fixed-camera videos (abandoned object, falling), 84 train / 41 test.
ServerQwen3.8-27B, Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, VideoLLaMA3-7B; readouts: generated JSON (D0), option-letter logits (D1); decision model: Laya typed-decisions zero-shot (D2) and fine-tuned on VALU (D3); controls: black frames, blank / shuffled description, options-only probe.
EdgeFrame differencing (+ EMA), Laya-Vision zero-shot and ETRI-tuned, four ETRI-trained CNNs, Qwen per frame (27B, 8B), VALU-trained MobileNetV3 (1 / 4 / 8 frames, 3 seeds, single-source), and students distilled from the VLM's typed readout on VALU and on ETRI footage.
Protocol32 uniform frames (long edge 672 px), BF16, greedy, one H200 (MIG slice for the small models); failures count as wrong. Edge thresholds at 1 % / 5 % frame false-positive budgets, chosen post-hoc and cross-fitted on video halves. End to end: windows −10 / +20 s, server P(yes) ≥ 0.5, GPU-seconds per hour of video.
