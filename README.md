# Surveillance Video Understanding Demo (static site)

Generated static site. Open the published page, or download and open `index.html` directly.

All measured scores are for **VALU demo subset v1 — adapted protocol**. They are not a
reproduction of the full VALU benchmark and are not comparable with paper scores.

Videos are derived previews of the publisher-masked UCF-Crime and ECVA videos released by the
VALU authors (https://huggingface.co/datasets/NIRCVAD/VALU). If you use them, cite VALU,
UCF-Crime and ECVA, and follow the terms of the original datasets.
